Dinner made simple.

SpoonDrop turns a screenshot, a photo or an Instagram reel into a recipe you can actually cook from — scaled to how many people are eating, with a shopping list that adds up.

What it does

  • Import a recipe from a screenshot, a photo, pasted text or an Instagram link.
  • Scan your fridge and see what you can cook without shopping first.
  • Plan the week and turn it into one shopping list, with quantities that scale to your household.
  • Cook step by step, with timers where the recipe needs them.
  • Follow creators and buy their recipes — they keep 75% of what you pay.

Coming to the App Store

SpoonDrop is in testing. If you would like to try it early, write to support@spoondrop.app.
