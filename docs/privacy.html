Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

1. Introduction

SpoonDrop ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and website.

2. Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Email address (for authentication)
  • Name (optional, for profile display)
  • Profile photo (optional)

Usage Data

  • Recipes you save, create, and cook
  • Shopping lists and meal plans
  • Family member information (stored in your account)

Photos and Media

When you upload recipe photos, they are stored securely in our cloud storage and used only to display them in the app.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the service
  • To personalize your experience
  • To process recipe imports using AI (text/images are sent to AI providers)
  • To process subscriptions and calculate creator earnings
  • To communicate with you about your account

4. Payments & Subscriptions

Purchases are processed by the Apple App Store and Google Play. We do not receive or store your card details. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription entitlements. Creator payouts, when enabled, are handled by Stripe.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — authentication and data storage
  • Apple & Google — sign-in and in-app purchases
  • RevenueCat — subscription management
  • Stripe — creator payouts
  • Google (Gemini) / Groq / OpenAI — AI recipe extraction

6. Data Security

  • HTTPS encryption for all data in transit
  • Secure authentication with JWT tokens
  • Row-level security in our database
  • No storage of payment card information

7. Your Rights

  • Access, correct, or export your personal data
  • Delete your account and all associated data — see Delete account (also available in the app under Settings)
  • Opt out of marketing communications

8. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected, to delete it, and to opt out of its sale. We do not sell your personal information.

9. Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time and will post the new version here with an updated date.
