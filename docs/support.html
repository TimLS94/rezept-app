Frequently asked questions

How do I cook a recipe?

Open a recipe and tap Start cooking. Cook Mode guides you step by step, with optional timers and photos. Check off steps as you go — finishing earns you a reward.

How do subscriptions work?

A subscription unlocks all premium recipes. It's billed through the App Store or Google Play and renews automatically until you cancel. You can start it from any premium recipe or from Settings → Subscription.

How do I cancel my subscription?
  • iPhone/iPad: Settings app → tap your name → Subscriptions → SpoonDrop → Cancel.
  • Android: Google Play → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → SpoonDrop → Cancel.

You keep access until the end of the current billing period.

How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

Sign in with the same account, then go to Settings → Subscription → Restore purchases.

I'm a creator — how do I earn money?

Publish free and premium recipes. Creators share 75% of net subscription revenue, split by how often their recipes are cooked by subscribers. Your live earnings and a full breakdown are in the app under Creator Studio → Earnings.

How do I change the servings / scale a recipe?

Use the servings stepper on a recipe, in Favorites, or in Cook Mode. Tap Family to scale to your household in one tap — ingredient amounts adjust automatically.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings → Delete Account. Or use our account deletion page. This permanently removes your account and data.
