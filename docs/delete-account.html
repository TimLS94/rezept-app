Delete your account

You can permanently delete your SpoonDrop account and its data at any time.

Option 1 — In the app (fastest)

  1. Open SpoonDrop and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings (Account & Settings).
  3. Scroll to Danger Zone and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm. Your account and data are removed immediately.

Option 2 — Request by email

Don't have the app installed? Email us from the address on your account and we'll delete it for you.

Send “Delete my account” to:

support@spoondrop.app

We verify the request comes from your account email and complete deletion within 30 days (usually much sooner).

What gets deleted

  • Your account and profile
  • Family members, favorites, collections, meal plans and shopping lists
  • Recipes you uploaded, and your cooking history & ratings

What may be retained

We may keep limited records required for legal, tax, or accounting reasons (for example, subscription/payout transaction records), and anonymized/aggregated data that no longer identifies you. Purchases and subscriptions are managed by the App Store or Google Play — cancel your subscription there separately.
